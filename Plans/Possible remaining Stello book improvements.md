# Possible remaining Stello book improvements

**I would keep Phase 7 small.** The v5 book already has useful moves, transposition handling and a working expansion tool. The most important change is not to grow it: **fix which book values v6 is allowed to call proven.** I found 41 solved labels inherited by trimming that the stored lines do not establish. After that, I would spend construction time on frequently reached book exits and judge the result on openings chosen independently of either book. I would not seed search cutoffs from ordinary book values.

## 1. A correctness issue to fix before the v6 rebuild

[BookMinimax](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookMinimax.cs#L33) takes the best **recorded** reply. That is a useful estimate for choosing book moves, but the opponent need not stay in the book. When a subtree is cut off, [the trimmer](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookTrimmer.cs#L205) walks the best recorded replies and copies the strongest leaf origin and search effort back to the severed edge. An exact draw at the end of that line is not an exact draw at its beginning. [One existing test](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine.Tests/BookTrimmerTests.cs#L70) explicitly expects a draw label to survive this operation.

**This affects the shipped data.** I replayed the [pre-trim book](https://github.com/cjp-dev/Stello/blob/3741e1344b5a941a1bc6ba598213a8a616e90b47/Stello.Net/Book/opening-book.txt), the [trim result](https://github.com/cjp-dev/Stello/blob/eb8cf76e3b20bd0ba2ba5a1cdb982500f5405219/Stello.Net/Book/opening-book.txt), and the [current master](../Stello.Net/Book/opening-book.txt#L1), matching positions by Stello's four symmetries and side to move, not by line spelling. The trim converted 373 backed-up edges: 332 to heuristic, **36 to exact and 5 to win/loss/draw (WLD)**. All 41 solved tags, values and efforts survive unchanged. That accounts for **36 of the current book's 60 exact leaves**.

For example, [the current `e1` entry](../Stello.Net/Book/opening-book.txt#L5597) claims `0:X:60s:d23:v5`. After `e1` there are **38 empty squares and 12 legal replies**. The [original node](https://github.com/cjp-dev/Stello/blob/3741e1344b5a941a1bc6ba598213a8a616e90b47/Stello.Net/Book/opening-book.txt#L4796) records only `f1` and `a3`. Its 23-ply solve metadata came from farther down the tree. As a check on all 41, I treated pre-trim exact leaves as point values, WLD leaves as outcome bounds, and unsearched alternatives as unconstrained; I negated child bounds and maximized over **all** legal replies, including forced passes. None of the 41 claimed labels follows from that evidence. **This is a provenance failure, not a finding that 41 game-theoretic scores are wrong.** No new solves were needed to distinguish the two.

**Change to the plan:** repair those 41 entries explicitly; an engine-version bump alone will not do it. [Recalc skips v5 exact entries](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookRecalculator.cs#L100) and [can retain a WLD value when a new search finishes only heuristically](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookRecalculator.cs#L69). Re-search the actual child without restricting its replies, or downgrade to an estimate with no proof tag, no solved-score sentinel and no borrowed effort. Going forward, keep a severed subtree's *ordering estimate*, not its descendant's certificate. Preserve genuine direct solves. Only propagate proofs when the bounds justify them: one winning reply proves at least a win for the mover; one drawing reply does **not** prove a draw if an omitted reply might win. Test both cases, including a forced pass.

## 2. Where additional book construction could pay

The [v5 experiments](making-stello-5-0-findings.md#L506) argue against a blanket increase in book size or search time. Re-ranking the old moves alone scored **49.9%**. Trimming, searching missing moves and expanding together produced a **smaller** book that scored **55.0%** on changed-move positions. On all ply-8 starts from the candidate book it scored **51.8% (49.7–54.0%)**. That is evidence for repairing decisions, not a measured gain for each construction step or proof of a general 55% win rate.

| Read-only check on the current 6,000-position book | Implication |
|---|---|
| **5,971** positions omit at least one legal reply; at **2,317** the preferred move leads straight to an unexpanded leaf. | Incompleteness is intentional. Rank exits by how often they occur and how much the decision could change; filling every legal move would spend most of the budget elsewhere. |
| **23** unlisted moves from known parents already lead to known positions. Only **one** has a backed-up estimate strictly above the parent's current first move. At [this position](../Stello.Net/Book/opening-book.txt#L1426), `c4` gives +4.37 estimated discs versus +3.56 for `c2`. | A useful, inexpensive `fill` candidate. Verify the two moves at comparable effort; +0.81 is not a proven improvement. [Calclib already links known children](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookCalclib.cs#L106), so measure what a final pass *after* DOE adds. |
| From book parents at plies 1–8, unlisted legal moves lead to **3,049 distinct off-book positions**; only **62** have an immediate reply into the book. Of **2,311 distinct exits** reached by taking a book parent's preferred leaf move, only **13** offer such a reply. | Try one-missing-position bridges offline before doing child lookups at every live miss. These are structural opportunities, not observed game frequencies. Being able to get back into the book says nothing about whether that move is good. |

**One additional experiment: spend a fixed budget on observed exits.** On a development set of games, record the first off-book board, side, played move and any return to book. Deduplicate transpositions. At frequently reached exits, compare the incumbent with the best omitted alternative at equal effort, then extend the lines where the choice changes or the score remains uncertain. Keep some ordinary [drop-out expansion (DOE)](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookExpander.cs#L522) for unfamiliar opponents. Compare this with standard DOE at equal search time and size on **different opening families**. Measure fewer costly exits and better moves after exit, not just a higher book-hit rate. Do not collect private user games to do this; local benchmark games or a permitted public corpus suffice.

## 3. What to keep, change or defer in Phase 7

| [Phase 7 proposal](making-stello-6-0-plan.md#L361) | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Variety / randomness** | **Keep as an opt-in playing feature, not an Elo claim.** Margin zero must preserve [the present policy](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/OpeningBook.cs#L98): a random symmetric first move for Black, then the first stored move, even on ties. Sample only after filtering for trustworthy outcomes and converting scores to like units. `Unknown` game-result markers can be ±32,665; an integer beyond `WinScore` is not itself a certificate. WLD wins do not give exact winning margins. For nonzero variety, test a per-side **cumulative** loss allowance so several individually small concessions cannot accumulate unchecked. Use seeded tests; keep the strong default unchanged. |
| **Child fallback / live unbooked-move null-window search** | **Try offline first.** A familiar child is an ordering hint, not a reason to play an inferior move. In particular, the [Egaroucid check](../Egaroucid-main/src/engine/ai.hpp#L612) searches *alternative moves for the mover*, not the opponent's unlisted refutation of the stored incumbent. To detect a stale incumbent, independently check that move as well. Stello [already tested](improve-stello-strength-findings.md#L482) `exit` (49.6%) and `all` (50.4%) at 100 ms; neither passed. A targeted ≥2 s test on stale or shallow entries is different. Share one deadline across verification and re-search; keep the legal incumbent on interruption. Account for the [three-miss tracker](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookTracker.cs#L5) and the [web book lookup before worker search](../Stello.Net/Stello.Web/Services/WebEngineHost.cs#L83). |
| **Pre-seed the search transposition table (TT)** | **Move hints first; no generic “free cutoffs.”** [Edax](../edax-reversi-master/src/book.c#L1142) seeds from a position it finds in its book, using its own level, depth and selectivity metadata. A literal probe at a Stello book miss finds no parent. Try a small list of legal moves from reachable known children; measure later hits, cutoff ordering, total latency and eviction of normal search entries. Never store a partial-book minimax value as both search bounds or invent a depth. Consider certified compatible bounds separately, after the provenance fix. |
| **Persistent best deviation; `fill`; uncertainty priority** | **Most useful construction work.** Cache the best *remaining* unbooked move with its canonical move mask, engine/evaluator version, effort and result kind. Invalidate it when the mask or search changes; an interrupted probe is not evidence that no better alternative exists. A severed played child is not automatically the best of all omitted moves. A tool-side cache can establish the value before changing the shipped format. Compare targeted `fill`/uncertainty against DOE at equal cost. |
| **Umigame numbers, A–F grades, hybrid threading** | **Optional diagnostics and build speed, not strength gates.** [Umigame](../Egaroucid-main/src/engine/umigame.hpp#L226) measures repertoire breadth under a play policy; [the grades](../Egaroucid-main/src/engine/book_accuracy.hpp#L30) summarize line/search coverage, not calibrated correctness. Report exposure, missing replies and direct versus inherited proofs first. Borrow idle cores for long tail jobs only within a global thread/RAM cap; compare wall time **and resulting book quality**, since a 60-second multithreaded search is not the same analysis as a single-threaded one. |
| **Rebuild, trim and expand with final v6** | **Keep.** Freeze v5 and checkpoint each stage. Pick the expansion budget explicitly: [the default cap is 6,000](../Stello.Net/tools/Stello.BookTool/SearchCommands.cs#L141), already today's book size. Full-width early play is a human-coverage option, not a reason to add weak moves to the engine's own repertoire. Compare its cost and exit coverage with targeted expansion before increasing the cap. |

## 4. Two changes to the release gate

1. **Measure strength on an independent book-on panel.** Keep the 500 ms, colour-swapped, same-engine A/B with variety off. Predeclare shallow openings from a source independent of v5/v6, or enumerate a balanced, symmetry-deduplicated shallow panel before constructing v6. Keep [changed-move starts](../Stello.Net/Stello.Engine/BookComparison.cs#L28) as a separate diagnostic: they exclude v5-only and v6-only positions. [`--starts-ply` currently samples book B](../Stello.Net/tools/Stello.BookTool/SearchCommands.cs#L262). The [587 engine-benchmark starts](../Stello.Net/tools/Stello.Benchmark/Data/starts-v6.txt#L1) also come from v5: playing the preferred v5 reply for both sides gives only **one consecutive book ply on 381/587** before the first miss, versus **22** from the position after `d3`. This measures initial exposure, not all eventual re-entries. Report paired score and interval, book moves per side, exits and time saved. If only the changed-move panel wins, say so.
2. **Verify the book actually delivered, including upgrades.** The tracked 114,169-byte binary passed `BookTool verify` against the text master. The *local generated* web copy was **193,210 bytes and failed that same comparison**. It is git-ignored and [copied during build](../Stello.Net/Stello.Web/Stello.Web.csproj#L161); this is **not** evidence that the live site serves the wrong book. Verify the final publish payload/hash, not only the source binary; the current binary uses **152,228 Base64 characters** before browser quota accounting. [Verifier warnings do not set its failure status](../Stello.Net/tools/Stello.BookTool/Program.cs#L129), so a zero-warning gate must check warnings as well as exit code. Exercise fresh install and saved-book upgrade: [web](../Stello.Net/Stello.Web/Services/StartupBook.cs#L18) and [desktop](../Stello.Net/Stello.Net/Services/AppPaths.cs#L20) prefer the user's book. Preserve learned games; do not silently overwrite them or assume an existing user is playing the new master.

**Bottom line:** correct the 41 unsupported proof labels, make the gate representative, and try targeted exit repair. If that does not beat v5 on independent openings, retaining the smaller, corrected book is a reasonable outcome. No book, engine, or plan changes were made for this review; no timed searches or matches were run.
